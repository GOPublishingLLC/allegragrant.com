Hey everyone, I’m Allegra, and I’m a crystal girl. I got started on this path a few years ago — after a few glasses of red wine — when I stumbled into a crystal shop in New York City, and well, my whole life changed. Everything I’ve learned since has gone into these books: crystals, essential oils, herbs, chakras, tarot, yoga, affirmations, and a little witchcraft. No mystique, no gatekeeping, nothing you need to already believe. Just what to do, and why it’s worth doing.
