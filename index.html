I’m not a mystic and I’m not a guru — I’m the friend who went down this rabbit hole, talked to everyone who would talk to me, tried all of it, and came back genuinely excited. My books are built around everyday rituals: crystals, essential oils, herbs, chakras, tarot, yoga, affirmations, and a little witchcraft. No mystique, no gatekeeping, nothing you need to already believe. Just what to do, and why it might be worth doing.
